Short answers

Is the rebate profit?

No. It’s IB commission handed back. The trade can still lose the spread, the swap, and the slippage. Don’t add the estimate on the card to your expected value and call it an edge.

Do losing trades still get paid?

Yes, when that volume earned IB. Win or lose is not in the formula.

When does the USDT show up?

Weekly, after Exness pays the IB. Not when you flatten the trade, and not because New York just closed. No IB statement, no transfer.

Why is Standard higher than Zero?

Standard and Pro pay a cut of the spread, so the “More” figures move. Zero/Raw gold (1.50 IB) and euro (0.43 IB) are fixed per lot at Common — 80% is 1.20 and 0.344. A tighter account usually means a smaller IB credit. Don’t price a Zero book off the Standard line.

What about a pair that isn’t under More?

Still 80% of whatever IB Exness pays on that lot. The published lines are only XAUUSD, EURUSD, and GBPUSD. Anything else is matched off the statement. No public figure here.

I already had an Exness account.

It isn’t on this rebate unless it was opened through the H79 link. Open a new account on the link. We don’t promise an IB transfer on the old one.

Does this cover Bulenox or Earn2Trade?

No. Those are futures prop programs on the trading fund page. FX Rebate currently pays an Exness account under the H79 partner link. CPT Markets is on this page, and its rebate is not open.
