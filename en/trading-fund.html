Building a trading fund

What we mean by a personal fund

A "fund" here is trading capital run with discipline: clear objectives, hard risk limits, a trade journal and regular reporting – instead of trading on gut feel. Job one isn't fast profits; it's protecting capital and showing the system has a positive expectancy over a meaningful sample.

Three pillars

  • System: written entry and exit rules, tested on historical data and then on a small live account.
  • Risk management: limits per trade, per day and per month.
  • Transparency: published results, ideally verified by a third party (Myfxbook, FXBlue, broker statements).

Bulenox

Futures

Futures prop: pass a Qualification Account, then activate Master / Funded — Ngọc Huy cleared the 50K program.

  • Qualification 50K (Option 1 – intraday trailing): $3,000 profit target; $2,500 max trailing drawdown (follows peak balance, open P&L included); up to 7 minis / 70 micros.
  • Min trading days: none — you can pass in a single day.
  • Consistency: not applied on Qualification.
  • Hours: flat by 3:59 PM CT; overnight holds are not allowed.
  • Option 2 (EOD): same $3,000 / $2,500 numbers with end-of-day trailing; daily loss limit on 50K is $1,100 (pauses the day; does not fail the account).

Topstep

Futures

Trading Combine® — simulated eval that unlocks an Express Funded Account®.

  • 50K Combine: $3,000 profit target; Maximum Loss Limit $2,000 (trails end-of-day balance; monitored in real time including open P&L).
  • Consistency: best day should stay under 55% of the profit target (going over raises the target).
  • Min days: cannot pass in one day — as few as two if consistency holds.
  • Daily Loss Limit: optional at purchase (50K: $1,000) — triggers a pause for the session, not a hard Combine failure.

Apex Trader Funding

Futures

Intraday trailing evaluation → simulated Performance Account (PA).

  • Intraday Evaluation 50K: $3,000 profit target; $2,000 intraday trailing max drawdown (follows Peak Balance, unrealized included).
  • Daily Loss Limit: none on the evaluation — risk is the trailing threshold only.
  • Consistency / scaling: not applied on evaluation (may apply later on the PA).
  • Min days: none — one-day pass allowed; 30 calendar days of access.

FTMO

Forex / CFD

Well-known CFD prop path: Challenge → Verification → FTMO Account (simulated trading + rewards).

  • 2-Step (standard): Challenge 10% profit → Verification 5% (of initial simulated capital).
  • Daily loss: max 5%/day; Maximum Loss static 10%.
  • Min trading days: 4 per phase.
  • 1-Step: 10% target; 3% daily; 10% EOD-trailing max loss; Best Day ≤ 50% of total profit (Consistency for accounts from 12 Oct 2026).
  • FTMO Account rewards: up to about 90% of simulated profit (per published plans).

FundedNext

Forex / CFD

Stellar CFD challenges (1-Step, 2-Step, Lite, Instant) with rules published before you trade.

  • Stellar 2-Step: Phase 1 8% → Phase 2 5%; 5% daily loss; static 10% max loss; 5 min days per phase.
  • Stellar 1-Step: 10% profit; 3% daily; static 6% max loss; 2 min days.
  • Time limit: none to pass; CFD accounts deactivate after 60 days with no trades.
  • Reward share: standard ~80%, scale-up to ~90% (optional add-on up to 95%).
Official site →

The5ers

Forex / CFD

Long-running CFD prop: 1-Step / Hyper Growth evaluation, then scaled Pro accounts.

  • Hyper Growth / 1-Step (example): 10% evaluation target; 6% stop-out / max loss; 3% daily loss.
  • Time limit: unlimited; inactive > 30 consecutive days may expire.
  • Level 1: no minimum trades or days required to complete (per Hyper Growth page).
  • Scales on ~10% milestones; published profit share starts around ~75% on early tiers.
