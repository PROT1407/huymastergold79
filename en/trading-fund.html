Building a trading fund

What we mean by a personal fund

A "fund" here is trading capital run with discipline: clear objectives, hard risk limits, a trade journal and regular reporting – instead of trading on gut feel. Job one isn't fast profits; it's protecting capital and showing the system has a positive expectancy over a meaningful sample.

Three pillars

  • System: written entry and exit rules, tested on historical data and then on a small live account.
  • Risk management: limits per trade, per day and per month.
  • Transparency: published results, ideally verified by a third party (Myfxbook, FXBlue, broker statements).

FTMO

Forex / CFD

Well-known CFD prop path: Challenge → Verification → FTMO Account (simulated trading + rewards).

  • Min trading days: 4 per phase.
  • 1-Step: 10% target; 3% daily; 10% EOD-trailing max loss; Best Day ≤ 50% of total profit (Consistency for accounts from 12 Oct 2026).
  • FTMO Account rewards: up to about 90% of simulated profit (per published plans).

Worked examples below illustrate pass / fail from published rules — not a forecast of returns.

Bulenox 50K

To pass: hit +$3,000 on Qualification without letting trailing DD chew through the $2,500 room (Option 1 intraday / Option 2 EOD), and be flat by 3:59 PM CT.

You fail when: equity tags the trailing floor; you hold overnight; or you exceed contract limits.

  • Pros: no min days; Option 2 daily pause instead of an instant fail.
  • Cons: $2,500 trailing is tight vs a $3,000 target; you must flat end of day.
  • Note: Ngọc Huy passed this path — certificate above; that doesn’t mean you will.

Topstep Combine 50K

To pass: +$3,000 while holding the $2,000 Maximum Loss Limit; keep your best day under the ~55% consistency guardrail.

You fail when: you tag the MLL, or consistency forces a higher target you never clear.

  • Pros: well-known Combine brand; optional daily loss only pauses the session.
  • Cons: no same-day pass; consistency can stretch the timeline.

Apex Intraday 50K

To pass: +$3,000 without letting Peak Balance drag the intraday trailing threshold through your $2,000 cushion.

You fail when: intraday trailing (open P&L included) hits the floor.

  • Pros: no daily loss / no consistency on eval; same-day pass allowed.
  • Cons: realtime trailing punishes early size; 30 calendar days of access.

Earn2Trade GAU50 / TCP50

To pass: +$3,000; stay above the $2,000 EOD DD and $1,100 daily loss; keep any single day under 30% of total PnL; respect the ladder (≤6 contracts at 50K); flat by 15:50 CT.

You fail when: daily loss, EOD DD, ladder breach, or trading outside approved hours.

  • Pros: EOD drawdown is easier to track than intraday trailing; 50K payout split is published (50/50 → 80/20); TCP offers a scale path.
  • Cons: $1,100 daily is a hard fail; 30% consistency forces profit across multiple days.

FTMO ~50K (2-Step)

To pass: Challenge +10%, then Verification +5%; ≥4 trading days each phase; stay inside 5% daily and 10% max loss.

You fail when: you breach daily or max loss in either phase.

  • Pros: familiar two-phase structure; published rewards up to ~90% on the FTMO Account.
  • Cons: two phases to clear; a static 10% max still blows up if you oversize.

FundedNext Stellar ~50K

To pass (2-Step): Phase 1 +8%, Phase 2 +5%; daily ≤5%, static max loss 10%; ≥5 days per phase.

You fail when: you hit daily or max loss, or leave a CFD account idle for 60 days.

  • Pros: 1-Step option (10% target, 6% max, 3% daily); reward share ~80–90%.
  • Cons: 2-Step takes longer; 1-Step daily/max are tighter.

The5ers Hyper Growth (example)

To pass: clear the 10% evaluation target without tagging the 6% stop-out or 3% daily loss.

You fail when: max loss / daily is hit, or the account sits inactive >30 consecutive days.

  • Pros: no min days at Level 1; published scale path on ~10% milestones.
  • Cons: 6% max and 3% daily are tighter than many 2-Step 10%/5% setups.
