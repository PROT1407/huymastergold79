What gold traders watch

  • FOMC / Fed funds rate: a hike or hawkish hold usually weighs on gold; cuts support it.
  • CPI: a hotter print lifts tightening bets → stronger dollar, gold often drops on the release.
  • Nonfarm payrolls: first Friday of the month, 8:30 AM ET – expect the biggest swings in the first 30 minutes.
  • PCE, GDP, ISM: move gold indirectly through rate expectations.
Tip: be flat or move stops to breakeven before tier-1 data, and let the first 5-minute candle close before acting.
