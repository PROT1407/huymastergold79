🔗 Gold vs. real yields vs. the dollar

XAU/USD (gold)
US 10-year note (price CFD)
DXY – US dollar index
Real yield (10-year TIPS) = nominal yield minus expected inflation. Gold pays no coupon, so higher real yields raise the cost of holding it – usually bearish. Latest: 2.91% (Oct 9, 2026).
Bond prices move opposite yields. The middle chart is the note's price: a rising price (falling yield) tends to move with gold.
A strong DXY usually weighs on dollar-priced gold, and vice versa. The inverse link isn't fixed – it weakens when central banks buy heavily or during geopolitical shocks.
