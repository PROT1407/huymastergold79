OilBullish for gold ↑🕐 Nov 4 U.S. settle — Vance comments during the U.S. day

WTI −8.3% settles at $88.12; Brent −8.7% at $94.62 — Vance blames Iran war for GOP midterm losses, market prices de-escalation

CNBC/Kitco: WTI lost 8.3% to settle at $88.12/bbl; Brent shed 8.7% to $94.62 — about a two-month low. VP JD Vance posted that “the reason we lost is the war”; Trump also tied gasoline/Iran to the midterm rout. Democrats’ dual-chamber win raised bets that Congress will squeeze war funding and push a Hormuz off-ramp. Crude is down more than 14% this week on U.S.–Iran deal chatter; Qatar is still mediating. CIBC’s Babin warned the selloff may be overdone; Rapidan’s McNally still leans escalation risk.

Gold impact: Near term: plunging oil eases energy-inflation pressure — that helps gold via lower expected yields. Longer term: there is still no Hormuz deal; recent U.S. strikes and ship attacks keep supply risk alive. Any Iran setback can reprice Brent and December hike odds higher again, and that would cap bullion.
