HSBC, J.P. Morgan and StoneX complete first live trades using Pooled Gold Interests (PGI)

Gold's digital evolution passed a major milestone after HSBC, J.P. Morgan and StoneX conducted wholesale-market transactions using Pooled Gold Interests (PGI) for the first time. Loco London physical 400-ounce Good Delivery gold bars were placed into the Wholesale Digital Gold ecosystem, and PGI were created.

Gold impact: Digitalization enhances gold's liquidity, allowing fractional ownership and more efficient use of physical gold as collateral across modern financial markets.
