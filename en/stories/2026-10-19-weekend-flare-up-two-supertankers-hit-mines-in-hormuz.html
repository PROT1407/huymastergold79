OilBearish for gold ↓🕐 Fri Oct 16 (UKMTO) · Sat Oct 17 (IRGC) · Sun Oct 18 (St. Petersburg) · Oct 18, 6:15 PM ET (CNBC)

Weekend flare-up: two supertankers hit mines in Hormuz, Ukraine strikes St. Petersburg oil terminal – Brent opens near $108.80

CNBC (6:15 PM ET Sunday): Brent shed $1.04 to $108.82 a barrel and WTI slipped 26 cents to $101.15, still hovering near recent highs. On Saturday the IRGC said two tankers, each carrying about 2 million barrels, hit mines and caught fire in the Strait of Hormuz after using a route Iran had not authorized (Al Jazeera/AFP). UKMTO reported two more tankers attacked off Saudi Arabia on Friday. On Sunday a wave of Ukrainian drones set an oil terminal and depot in St. Petersburg on fire; the terminal exports about 120,000 b/d of refined products, per Kpler. Last week Brent gained 4.8% and WTI 10.9% (Investing.com).

Gold impact: Near term: last week proved that higher oil isn't helping gold. Pricier crude feeds inflation, revives Fed hike bets and caps bullion; Investing.com noted oil's gains outweighed the softer dollar. If Brent reclaims $110, watch for selling into $4,200. Longer term: as long as Hormuz stays choked, energy inflation keeps yields elevated, and only a reopening of oil flows takes that pressure off and gives gold room to break out.
