Gold struggles near one-year lows, silver rebounds as stocks slide, Fed hike bets persist - Kitco PM Report

Spot gold was trading near $3,853.10 an ounce, down $2.40, or 0.06%, while silver was trading near $47.330, up $0.472, or 1.01%, on the session. Gold traded between $3,836.00 and $3,891.60 during the day. October retail sales increased 0.2%, and industrial production declined 0.4%. The latest published futures assessments indicated approximately a 94% probability of a 25-basis point increase.

Gold impact: In the short term, expectations of further Federal Reserve tightening and elevated Treasury yields continue to cap precious metals. In the long term, ongoing geopolitical tensions provide a floor for gold, though a sustained recovery depends on an eventual shift in monetary policy.
