Cross-assetBearish for gold ↓🕐 Oct 26, 11:21 PM ET (CNBC)

Gold slips back to $4,000 with the dollar near a one-week high – FedWatch prices 82% odds of a December hike

CNBC (Reuters): spot gold eased 0.2% to $4,007.29 an ounce by 11:21 PM ET Monday, and December futures fell 0.5% to $4,019.70. The dollar held near a one-week high, making bullion pricier for overseas buyers. Tastylive's Ilya Spivak says gold is digesting inside Friday's range after last week's big drop – and a break through $3,900 could open a slide toward $3,800. CME FedWatch: about 11% odds of a hike this week and 82% for December. By 2:40 AM ET, CNBC XAU= was 3,999.67, DXY 102.10 and silver down 2.2%.

Gold impact: Near term: a firmer dollar and December hike bets keep a lid on gold; $4,000 is the pivot, and bounces into 4,014–4,020 are likely to get sold ahead of the Fed. Longer term: high real rates are still the main headwind – gold needs a less hawkish Fed or a weaker dollar to get its uptrend back.
