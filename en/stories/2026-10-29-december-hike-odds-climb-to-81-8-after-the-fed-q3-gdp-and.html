FedNeutral🕐 Oct 28, 7:05 PM ET (Investing.com) – GDP/PCE Oct 29, 8:30 AM ET

December hike odds climb to 81.8% after the Fed – Q3 GDP and September PCE hit at 8:30 AM ET

Investing.com's Fed Rate Monitor (CME 30-day fed funds futures, 7:05 PM ET Wednesday): for Dec. 8–9, 81.8% odds of a move to 4.00%–4.25% (72.8% a day earlier) vs. 18.2% for a hold, with a 50 bp hike priced out. Bloomberg (via FXStreet) says traders kept the December bet but trimmed wagers on further hikes in 2027. WSJ: GDP and PCE, the Fed's preferred inflation gauge, are both due Thursday.

Gold impact: Near term: a hot core PCE would make December a near-lock and open a break below $4,000; a cooler print gives gold a shot at 4,080–4,100. Longer term: the market is pricing out an extended 2027 hiking run – if that continues, rate pressure on gold should ease over time.
