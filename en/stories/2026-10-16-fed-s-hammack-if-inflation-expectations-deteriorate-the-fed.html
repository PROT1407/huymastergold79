FedBearish for gold ↓🕐 Oct 15, 5:38 PM ET (Reuters)

Fed's Hammack: if inflation expectations deteriorate, the Fed may need to hike more aggressively

Reuters (5:38 PM ET): speaking at a Goldman Sachs event in New York, Cleveland Fed President Beth Hammack, an FOMC participant and early hawk, warned that if an 'inflationary mindset' sets in, 'we might need to move even more.' She gave no firm guidance for the Oct. 27–28 meeting but said financial conditions look roughly neutral, with no real restraint despite higher borrowing costs. Her remarks were among the last before the pre-meeting blackout.

Gold impact: Near term: hawkish Fedspeak keeps hike bets alive, a mild negative for gold, though the tape is focused on Warsh. Longer term: if inflation expectations drift higher, the Fed has to do more, lifting real yields and pressuring bullion.
