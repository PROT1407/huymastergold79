FedWatch after Warsh: October hold odds climb to 83.9%, December hike still ~88% – Fed enters its blackout through Oct 29

Investing.com's Fed Rate Monitor (CME fed funds futures, updated Oct 17, 12:35 AM ET): Oct 28 meeting – hold at 3.75%–4.00% 83.9%, 25 bp hike 16.1% (81.6%/18.4% a week earlier); Dec 9 meeting – 4.00%–4.25% 74.1%, 4.25%–4.50% 13.7%, no change 12.2%. In Bangkok on Friday, Chair Warsh said "I don't prejudge meetings," noting only that the Fed "removed a dose of accommodation" in September. BNY's John Velis sees too little support on the committee, especially among governors, for an October hike, but calls December an open question. The pre-FOMC blackout started Oct 17, so no Fed officials will talk policy this week.

Gold impact: Near term: an October hike is essentially off the table, which helps gold and weighs on the dollar. With the Fed silent, price action will key off oil, yields and AI stocks. Longer term: the market still has December penciled in (UBS also sees another hike in March 2027), and higher-for-longer remains gold's biggest headwind.
