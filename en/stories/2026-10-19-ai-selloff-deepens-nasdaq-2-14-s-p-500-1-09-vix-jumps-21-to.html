AI selloff deepens: Nasdaq −2.14%, S&P 500 −1.09%, VIX jumps 21% to about 20 as stress spreads to credit

AP: on Friday the S&P 500 fell 1.09% to 7,659.44, the Nasdaq dropped 2.14% to 26,140.03 and the Dow lost 0.34% to 52,002.41. Investing.com: the VIX jumped 21.45% to 19.99. CNBC (Reuters) says the AI-stock pain spread to parts of the credit market on worries data center operators won't turn a profit in time to service their debt; Investing.com notes those AI-debt fears are what restored Treasuries' safe-haven role. Yahoo Finance at 7:24 PM ET Sunday: S&P 500 futures (ES=F) at 7,733.

Gold impact: Near term: risk-off is flowing into bonds and pulling yields down, which indirectly supports gold. If the selling turns into a liquidity squeeze, though, gold can get sold to cover margin, as in past panics. Longer term: AI-debt risk is a fresh reason for big money to diversify into gold.
