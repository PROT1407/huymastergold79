FedNeutral🕐 Oct 22, 7:15 PM ET

FedWatch: October hike odds down to 8%; December hike pricing eases to about 75% from roughly 87%

Investing.com's Fed Rate Monitor (CME 30-day fed funds futures, 7:15 PM ET Oct 22): Oct. 28 meeting – 92.0% hold at 3.75%–4.00%, 8.0% for a 25 bp hike (13.8% a day earlier, 28.7% a week ago). Dec. 9 meeting – 25.2% hold (13.2% a day earlier), 69.0% for 4.00%–4.25% and 5.8% for 4.25%–4.50%. Reuters, citing CME FedWatch, put October hike odds at 7.2%, down from 27.7% a week ago. The Fed is in its blackout through Oct. 29; September PCE is due Oct. 29.

Gold impact: Near term: hike bets are cooling with yields, but $113 Brent can flip that quickly – gold hasn't caught a bid from it yet. Longer term: roughly three-in-four odds of a December hike remain; pricing below 50% is what would signal a trend change for gold.
