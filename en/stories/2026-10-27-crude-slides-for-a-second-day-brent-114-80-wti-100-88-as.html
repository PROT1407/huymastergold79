War & geopoliticsNeutral🕐 Oct 27, 1:09 AM ET (CNBC)

Crude slides for a second day – Brent $114.80, WTI $100.88 – as Tehran talks revive hopes of cooling the U.S.-Iran war

CNBC: by 1:09 AM ET, Brent was down 0.87% at $114.80 a barrel and WTI down 0.86% at $100.88 after Saudi, Turkish and Pakistani officials met Iranian leaders in Tehran and agreed to keep talking. President Trump says he's close to deciding between a deal with Iran and stepped-up military action. The drop is capped by ongoing Houthi attacks on Saudi Arabia, and October has been the worst month for shipping attacks around the Strait of Hormuz.

Gold impact: Near term: cooler crude eases the inflation scare, but not enough to pull yields or the dollar lower – so gold isn't getting the benefit. A bad Hormuz or Houthi headline could send oil right back up. Longer term: the war is still live – an inflation risk that hurts gold through rates, but also a haven bid on any surprise escalation.
