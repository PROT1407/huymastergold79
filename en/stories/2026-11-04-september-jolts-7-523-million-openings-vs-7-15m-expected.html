FedBearish for gold ↓🕐 JOLTS 10:00 AM ET Nov 3 — Investing.com FedWatch 6:05 PM ET Nov 3

September JOLTS: 7.523 million openings (vs ~7.15M expected); FedWatch (Investing.com/CME) as of Nov 3 close: 77.4% chance of a 25 bp December hike / 22.6% hold

The openings rate climbed to 4.5% (+0.2 ppt). Hires ~5.4 million; quits ~3.37 million; layoffs ~1.64 million. September factory orders +1.8% (vs ~1.2%–1.3% expected). Investing.com Fed Rate Monitor (CME futures), 6:05 p.m. ET Nov 3: Dec 9 — hold 22.6% (prior day 26.2%); hike to 4.00%–4.25% 77.4% (prior day 73.8%). Jan 27, 2027: hold 16.1%; 4.00%–4.25% 61.7%; 4.25%–4.50% 22.1%. CNBC’s intraday FedWatch print sat near 73% (down from 76% earlier Tuesday).

Gold impact: Near term: hot labor and factory data lifted December hike odds — gold gets hit through yields and the dollar. Longer term: a soft ADP on Wednesday or a soft NFP on Friday can reopen the hold case quickly; another hot print locks in selling pressure into the $3,975–$4,000 resistance band.
