War & geopoliticsNeutral🕐 Araghchi Oct 18, ~7:06 AM ET · CBS update Oct 18, 3:02 PM ET

Iran says Hormuz could reopen 'on the sixth day' if the US meets its terms; UKMTO counts at least 10 ships attacked in a week – Trump eyes a deal after Nov. 3

CBS News (Sunday): Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi said Tehran is still reviewing US "comments" on its seven-day ceasefire proposal from September, which calls for lifting the US blockade of Iranian ports, removing oil sanctions and a ceasefire on all fronts; if Washington complies, Hormuz reopens on day six and talks begin on day seven. UKMTO says at least 10 vessels have been attacked in or near the strait since last Sunday, a weekly high since the war began in February, with 23 attacks so far this month. Per Al Jazeera, President Trump ruled out nuclear weapons and ground troops and said he expects a deal to end the war after the Nov. 3 midterms.

Gold impact: Near term: there's a diplomatic opening, but nothing binding, while ship attacks are rising, so headline risk cuts both ways and gold can whip around on the news. Longer term: a peace deal, meaning cheaper oil, cooler inflation and fewer Fed hikes, is the most bullish setup for gold, and the post-Nov. 3 window is worth watching.
