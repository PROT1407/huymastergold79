FedBullish for gold ↑🕐 Williams: Oct 13, 2:21 PM ET; Waller: Oct 13, 6:57 AM ET; gold measured 2:00–3:30 PM ET Oct 13

Fed's Williams (NY Fed, voter): one more hike this year is appropriate, no need seen for 2027 – DXY slips to ~102.2

FXStreet (2:21 PM ET Oct 13): New York Fed President John Williams said disinflationary forces are still at work, the labor market isn't adding to inflation, and price pressure is coming mainly from AI and energy; 'one more rate increase this year is appropriate' and he currently sees no need to hike in 2027. The same day, Governor Christopher Waller (Reuters, 6:57 AM ET) said rising Treasury yields reflect real growth, AI investment and debt concerns – not inflation expectations, which remain 'well anchored'. Dollar: DXY on CNBC 102.215 (−0.36%), day range 102.162–102.574, backing off an 18-month high. COMEX gold (GC=F, Yahoo, 5-min bars): 2:00 PM ET 4,174.5 → 2:05 PM 4,182.1 → 2:55 PM 4,186.5 → 3:30 PM 4,188.8.

Gold impact: Near term: a 'one more and done' message cooled the dollar and COMEX gold bounced about $10–14 over the next 90 minutes. Bigger picture: if the Fed confirms a terminal rate of 4.00–4.25% with no hikes in 2027, rate pressure on gold should fade over time; but Collins and Hammack are still hawkish, so today's CPI is the real test.
