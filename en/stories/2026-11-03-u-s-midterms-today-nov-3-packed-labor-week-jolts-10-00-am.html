FedNeutral🕐 Election Day Nov 3 — JOLTS 10:00 AM ET today — NFP 8:30 AM ET Friday, Nov 6

U.S. midterms today (Nov 3); packed labor week: JOLTS 10:00 AM ET today — ADP/ISM Services Wednesday — NFP 8:30 AM ET Friday

Election Day is Tuesday, Nov 3. CNBC/Julius Baer: U.S. deficits are unlikely to shift enough post-vote to rewrite gold’s longer-term fiscal support, but the congressional balance can still jolt near-term fiscal expectations, the dollar and yields. Kitco’s week-ahead: September JOLTS and factory orders at 10:00 a.m. ET today; ADP at 8:15 a.m. ET and ISM Services at 10:00 a.m. ET Wednesday; claims and productivity Thursday; October NFP at 8:30 a.m. ET Friday, Nov 6. Gold impact: Near term: early election prints and JOLTS can whip the dollar and yields from Asia through New York — better to watch than chase. Longer term: Friday’s NFP is the print most likely to move FedWatch and set gold’s tone for the rest of the week.
