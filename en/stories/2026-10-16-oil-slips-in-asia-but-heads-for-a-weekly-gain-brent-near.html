Oil slips in Asia but heads for a weekly gain: Brent near $105.20, WTI near $97.20 – Hormuz flows running at about 68% of normal

Reuters (via Euronext, 11:52 PM ET): Brent fell 0.67% to $105.21 a barrel and WTI dropped 0.79% to $97.20; for the week, Brent is up 0.44% and WTI 5.81%. Kpler: Hormuz flows averaged 11.62 million bpd in the seven days to Oct 13, about 68% of the 17.15 million bpd baseline. The IEA agreed to release the remaining 100 million barrels of emergency stocks, including 20 million barrels of diesel, and the Trump administration is weighing lifting sanctions on some 'shadow fleet' tankers to bring down record freight rates (CNBC). ING says it will take a US–Iran deal to fully strip out the risk premium. Yahoo Finance at 2:32 AM ET: Brent (BZ=F) at $105.18.

Gold impact: Near term: softer crude trims gasoline-driven inflation worries, a small plus for gold through the yield channel. Longer term: Brent stuck above $100 keeps US inflation hot and the Fed leaning toward hikes, a headwind, while Hormuz supply risk keeps a floor under haven demand – the tug-of-war continues.
