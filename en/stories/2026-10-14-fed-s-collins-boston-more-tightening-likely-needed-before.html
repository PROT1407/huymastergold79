Fed's Collins (Boston): more tightening likely needed before end-2026 – COMEX gold slips ~$5 to around 4,181 after her remarks

Boston Fed President Susan Collins spoke 4:00–4:45 PM ET Oct 13 at the Boston Fed's regional banking conference. Reuters (4:01 PM ET): she backed September's hike to 3.75–4.00% and now thinks 'it would be appropriate to raise rates somewhat further before year-end to ensure inflation returns to the 2% target in a timely and sustainable way'. Timing depends on incoming data. She was upbeat on GDP growth and a labor market near full employment, but warned headline and core inflation will run above 2% for the next few months on refined fuels (jet, diesel, heating oil), AI investment, tariffs and the Middle East war. No same-day CNBC story; the full text wasn't on the Boston Fed site yet. Gold reaction (COMEX GC=F, Yahoo, 5-min bars, ET): 1:00 PM 4,176.1 · 3:00 PM 4,185.9 · 3:30 PM 4,188.8 · 4:00 PM (start) 4,186.2 · 4:15 PM 4,183.0 · 4:20 PM low 4,180.7 · 4:30 PM 4,180.8 · 4:40 PM 4,181.2. Spot (gold-api.com) $4,160.30 at ~4:51 PM ET. DXY 102.19 (3:30 PM) → 102.20 (4:00 PM) → 102.21 (4:40 PM). The 10-year (^TNX) parked around 5.217% at 2:55 PM ET (Treasury session already thin).

Gold impact: Clearly hawkish: Collins confirmed one more hike before end-2026 (the market leans December over the Oct 27–28 meeting). In theory, more Fed tightening plus high yields is bearish for gold near term – and in practice GC=F fell about $5–8 (4,186 → ~4,181) within 20–40 minutes of her start. Bigger picture: if today's 8:30 AM ET CPI runs hot, December hike odds climb and gold stays under pressure; if CPI is soft, her remarks matter less since she doesn't vote this year.
