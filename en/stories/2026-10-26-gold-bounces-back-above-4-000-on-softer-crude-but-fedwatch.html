FedBearish for gold ↓🕐 Oct 26, 2:08 AM ET (CNBC)

Gold bounces back above $4,000 on softer crude, but FedWatch still shows 75% odds of a December hike

CNBC (Reuters): spot gold rose 0.4% to $4,009.87 an ounce at 11:42 PM ET Sunday after hitting its lowest since mid-July in the prior session and dropping 4% last week. Capital.com's Kyle Rodda called the bounce mostly a relief move on lower oil that "could easily be capped" ahead of the FOMC. The market expects a hold this week, with December hike odds at 75% per CME FedWatch; PCE lands the day after the meeting.

Gold impact: Near term: there's no new haven bid behind this bounce, so 4,019–4,025 is close resistance. Longer term: December hike odds and a 10-year near 5% are still the main weight on gold; it takes a less hawkish Fed to turn the trend.
