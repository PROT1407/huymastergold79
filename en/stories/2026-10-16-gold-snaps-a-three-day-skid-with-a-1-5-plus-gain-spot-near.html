GoldBullish for gold ↑🕐 Oct 15, 4:37 PM ET (Kitco, NY close) · 7:48 PM ET (Kitco, early Asia)

Gold snaps a three-day skid with a 1.5%-plus gain: spot near $4,168.70 at the close, easing to ~$4,155.70 in early Asia

Kitco (4:37 PM ET): spot gold near $4,168.70, up $64.20 or 1.56%, with silver near $57.31 (+3.24%) as falling Treasury yields, a softer dollar and renewed haven demand unwound Wednesday's CPI selloff. Reuters (via CNBC) had gold up more than 1%, snapping three straight losses; Zaner Metals' Peter Grant said the market largely priced in the hot PPI and sees the Fed 'hamstrung to some degree' by the fiscal picture. December COMEX gold closed at $4,170.10 (CNBC). In early Asian trade, Kitco had spot at $4,155.70 at 7:48 PM ET, down $14.10 (−0.34%) from the New York close, as traders wait on Warsh.

Gold impact: Near term: the 10-year yield's biggest daily drop since August 2025 and the dollar's retreat from an 18-month high did the heavy lifting. Kitco has resistance at $4,161.50–$4,181.40 and support at $4,127.60, then $4,107.30; a hawkish Warsh at 11:30 PM ET could cap the bounce at resistance. Longer term: sticky inflation keeps the risk of higher real yields alive, a headwind, while the Iran war keeps a floor under haven demand.
