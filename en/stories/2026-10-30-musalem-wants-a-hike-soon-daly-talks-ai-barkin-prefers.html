FedBearish for gold ↓🕐 Oct 30, 9:30 AM ET (Barkin) – noon ET (Daly) – 12:30 PM ET (Musalem)

Musalem wants a hike 'soon,' Daly talks AI, Barkin prefers gradual – Fed leans hawkish, gold flat near $3,900

Musalem (St. Louis Fed, 12:30 PM ET, CNBC/Reuters): "I judge that additional firming of monetary policy will be necessary, and soon"; "incremental adjustments made earlier are usually less disruptive than larger and abrupt actions taken later"; inflation is "too high," the economy "resilient." Daly (San Francisco Fed, noon ET, investingLive): AI matters for policy in the near and medium term, for both the price-stability and employment mandates — no rate signal. Barkin (Richmond Fed, 9:30 AM ET radio, Bloomberg via Yahoo Finance): direction of travel is "straightforward," the question is pace, and "a gradual approach is a good way to go"; AI investment is fueling demand-side inflation. Versus our call: Musalem hawkish — right; Daly neutral — right. Market barely moved: spot gold near $3,899 at 12:32 PM ET (Why Gold Moved), December futures flat at $3,905–$3,910 through the remarks (Yahoo Finance); DXY ~101.9; 10-year yield ~4.95% (down 4 bps on the day). CME FedWatch ~75% odds of a December hike (FXStreet).

Gold impact: The hawkish message was already in the price — gold had dropped nearly 3% before anyone spoke, so Musalem confirmed rather than triggered. The plan doesn't change: no bottom-fishing while price sits below $3,950–$4,000, favor selling bounces, and a break of $3,890 opens $3,850. The two hawkish dissenters, Cook and Hammack, haven't published their explanations yet — worth watching. Trade the plan, not the noise.
