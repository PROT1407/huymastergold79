YieldsBullish for gold ↑🕐 Oct 16 close · CNBC Oct 16, 5:20 PM ET · Yahoo Oct 18, 7:24 PM ET

10-year real yield slips to 2.74%, dollar posts its first weekly loss in five weeks with DXY at 101.87

U.S. Treasury (Oct 16 close): the 10-year yield ended at 5.09% (5.10% prior), the 2-year at 4.74% (from 4.77%) and the 30-year at 5.43%; the 10-year TIPS real yield fell to 2.74%, from 2.78% the day before and 2.91% on Oct 9. CNBC (Reuters): the dollar index lost 0.17% to 101.87 for a 0.35% weekly drop, its first weekly decline in more than a month. StoneX's Matt Weller notes the dollar is trading with the AI theme: tech selling is sending money into bonds, not the greenback. Yahoo Finance at 7:24 PM ET Sunday: DXY at 101.89.

Gold impact: Near term: a 17 bp drop in real yields in one week is the main reason gold held up despite higher oil, and it's the number to watch. StoneX's Daniel Pavilonis calls gold an inverse 10-year yield trade and says a break below 5% on the 10-year would be a clear positive. Longer term: nominal yields near 5% are still restrictive, and if inflation forces more Fed hikes, a rebound in yields would drag gold lower.
