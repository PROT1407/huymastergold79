GoldNeutral🕐 Oct 16, 5:15 PM ET close (Investing.com) · Kitco survey Oct 16, 9:17 PM ET · Oct 18, 7:24 PM ET (Yahoo GC=F)

Gold futures open the week near $4,201 in Asia after a sixth down week in eight – the $4,200 ceiling is the test again

Friday's close (Investing.com): spot gold slipped 0.1% to $4,165.40/oz while futures added 0.4% to $4,186.70; both fell 0.7% on the week. Dow Jones Market Data shows front-month Comex gold down in six of the past eight weeks and still 21.8% below its Jan. 29 record of $5,318.40. Per Kitco, gold ran from a $4,216.91 high on Monday to a $4,073.35 low at midday Wednesday after CPI, then rallied back to just under $4,200 and stalled. Kitco's weekly survey: Wall Street 33% bullish, 17% bearish, 50% neutral (12 analysts); Main Street 43% bullish, 45% bearish. Yahoo Finance at 7:24 PM ET Sunday: GC=F at $4,201.30.

Gold impact: Near term: gold is still boxed in roughly $4,075–$4,220, and the Sunday open puts it right back at $4,200. Without a solid close above that area it stays a level to sell rallies into; a clean break opens $4,220 and up. Support to hold is $4,150–$4,155, then $4,100. Bigger picture: Forex.com's James Stanley calls $4,000 the line that matters for the trend, and Adrian Day sees the end of the war, with a weaker dollar, cheaper oil and fading hike bets, as the catalyst for a real breakout.
