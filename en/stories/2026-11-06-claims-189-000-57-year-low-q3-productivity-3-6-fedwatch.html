Richmond’s Barkin: a gradual hike pace “makes sense.” Markets await October NFP Friday 8:30 AM ET — Dow Jones ~59,000 / Reuters ~65,000; unemployment ~4.2%.

Gold impact: Near term: December hike odds crashing from ~82% to ~60% is the clearest gold tailwind of the session — even with firm claims and a still-hawkish Hammack. Bigger picture: Friday’s payrolls/wages decide it. A hot print can re-spike hike odds and yields and clip the $4,100–$4,150 bounce; a soft one cements the hold path and opens a clean test of higher resistance.
