Cross-assetNeutral🕐 Oct 15, 4:00 PM ET (NYSE close)

Wall Street climbs off the lows: Dow +1.08% to 52,177.69, S&P 500 +0.33%, Nasdaq +0.08% as money rotates out of AI; Asia set to open higher

CNBC: the Dow added 556.21 points (+1.08%) to 52,177.69, the S&P 500 gained 0.33% to 7,744.06 and the Nasdaq rose 0.08% to 26,713.03 after being down as much as 1.4% intraday, as worries about AI overspending and debt pushed investors from tech into value. Investing.com: VIX 16.46 (−2.43%). Bloomberg (via The Edge Malaysia): Japan, South Korea and Australia equity futures point to gains after a four-day slide, and the equal-weight S&P 500 rose 1.4%.

Gold impact: Near term: steadier risk appetite can drain haven flows from bonds and let yields bounce, a slight negative for gold; a renewed AI rout would push yields lower and support bullion. Longer term: the debt load behind the AI buildout is a new systemic risk that can support gold as a defensive asset.
