YieldsBullish for gold ↑🕐 Oct 29, ~4:00 PM ET (close)

Yields slip back under 5% and the dollar eases to ~101.8 as tech gets hit – but gold doesn't catch the bid

Newsquawk (settlement): the 2-year fell 3.1 bp to 4.654%, the 10-year 1.9 bp to 4.984% and the 30-year finished at 5.324% as the curve steepened; a solid 7-year auction helped. CNBC had the 10-year as low as 4.964% intraday. Investing.com showed the dollar index at 101.76 (−0.27%) in the evening. Stocks: S&P 500 −0.3% to 7,720.39 and Nasdaq −1.44% as Alphabet dropped about 13% on earnings and AI-spending worries (Kitco). Kitco's read: lower yields and a softer dollar still couldn't spark safe-haven buying in metals.

Gold impact: Near term: a 10-year back under 5% and a softer dollar are supportive; hold that for a few sessions and gold has a case to defend the $4,000 area. Longer term: gold falling even as yields and the dollar ease says the market is pricing a long higher-for-longer cycle – a weak tell. It will take a much deeper drop in yields to turn the trend.
