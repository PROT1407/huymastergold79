War & geopoliticsBearish for gold ↓🕐 Oct 29 → ~2:30 PM ET (crude settlement)

Washington reaches out to Tehran via Qatar on restarting talks – crude eases: Brent $114.88, WTI $102.00

Vice President JD Vance sent a letter through Qatari mediators to Mohsen Rezaei, head of Iran's Supreme National Security Council, proposing to restart peace talks; Qatar also tabled a separate draft deal that a senior Iranian official called unacceptable (Kitco, Al Jazeera). Tehran has not formally responded. CNBC/Reuters: Brent settled down 44 cents (−0.38%) at $114.88 a barrel and WTI down 78 cents (−0.76%) at $102.00, after both were off more than $2 intraday; WTI touched $99.79 (DTN). December Brent expires Friday and OPEC+ meets Sunday.

Gold impact: Near term: talk of talks takes some heat out of crude and the energy-inflation story, but it also drains haven demand, so gold gets little out of it. Longer term: the gap between Washington and Tehran on sanctions and Hormuz is still wide and escalation risk hasn't gone away; oil in the $100–$115 range keeps inflation and rates elevated.
