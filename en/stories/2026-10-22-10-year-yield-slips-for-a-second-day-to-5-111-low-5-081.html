YieldsBullish for gold ↑🕐 Oct 21, 1:00 PM ET (20-year auction) · 4:00 PM ET (stock close)

10-year yield slips for a second day to 5.111% (low 5.081%); mixed 20-year auction; Nasdaq jumps 1.05% on SpaceX

Reuters (via Mint): the 10-year yield fell 0.6 basis point to 5.111%, the 30-year was at 5.412% and the 2-year at 4.789%; the $13 billion 20-year sale stopped at 5.469%, tailing the when-issued, though the 2.73 bid-to-cover was above average. The 5-year TIPS breakeven sat at 2.41%, a five-week high. Treasury: the 10-year real yield was 2.76%. CNBC: the S&P 500 rose 0.58% to 7,757.17, the Nasdaq gained 1.05% to 26,702.86 and the Dow added 0.69% to 52,856.09 as SpaceX jumped 7%. Investing.com: DXY 101.52 (−0.08%), VIX 16.68.

Gold impact: Near term: slightly lower yields and a softer dollar are keeping gold above 4,100, but a strong equity tape drains haven demand – hard for gold to break out. Thursday brings a $26 billion 5-year TIPS auction. Longer term: a 10-year real yield near 2.76% is still very restrictive and caps gold's upside.
