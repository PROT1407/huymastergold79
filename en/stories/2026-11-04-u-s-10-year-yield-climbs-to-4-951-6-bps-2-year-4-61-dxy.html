YieldsBearish for gold ↓🕐 Nov 3 U.S. session — yields/Kitco into the U.S. close; DXY Reuters intraday

U.S. 10-year yield climbs to ~4.951% (+~6 bps), 2-year ~4.61%, DXY near 101.83 — hot JOLTS and factory orders into election day

Kitco/MarketWatch: the U.S. 10-year yield rose about 5.8–6 bps to ~4.951%; the 30-year ~5.323% (+~5.5 bps); the 2-year ~4.612% (+~3 bps). CNBC’s intraday wrap had the 10-year near 4.96%, the 30-year near 5.332% and the 2-year near 4.623%. Reuters: the dollar index edged 0.07% higher to 101.83 after stronger-than-expected JOLTS and September factory orders (+1.8%). Equities mixed: Dow −0.38% (51,786.72), S&P 500 +0.36% (7,568.92), Nasdaq +0.83% (25,372.01) — selective semis bounce, Apple soft. Markets are still pricing Congress risk and the rest of the labor week.

Gold impact: Near term: higher yields and a firmer dollar are the direct gold headwind — they cut Monday’s rebound short. Longer term: if Democrats take the House (the pre-election base case), fiscal flexibility shrinks and longer yields could ease — that would help bullion. But hot JOLTS/NFP keeps December hike odds elevated, so any gold bounce still has to clear the real-yield bar.
