YieldsBearish for gold ↓🕐 Oct 23, 3:00 PM ET (Tradeweb marks) · 4:00 PM ET (stock close)

10-year yield bounces 5 bps to 4.999% as crude tops $100 but still falls 11 bps on the week; S&P 500 hits a five-week low

CNBC: the 10-year yield rose 5 bps to 4.999%, the 2-year gained 5 bps to 4.686% and the 30-year added 4 bps to 5.312%. Chris Rupkey of Fwdbonds said WTI closing above $100 means "more inflation is on the way," so yields are adjusting higher. Dow Jones Market Data (Tradeweb 3 PM ET marks): the 10-year was at 4.987%, down 0.110 percentage point on the week – a third straight weekly decline. CNBC quotes: S&P 500 −0.52% to 7,623.68, Nasdaq −0.21% to 25,955.34, VIX 19.45; DXY around 101.75.

Gold impact: Near term: yields bouncing with crude was a direct headwind for gold on Friday. Longer term: the 10-year is well off its Oct. 5 high of 5.31% and down three weeks running – if the oil shock fades, a lower yield floor is what lets gold rebuild.
