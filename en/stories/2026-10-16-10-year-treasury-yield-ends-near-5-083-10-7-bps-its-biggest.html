YieldsBullish for gold ↑🕐 Oct 15, 4:31 PM ET (CNBC quotes)

10-year Treasury yield ends near 5.083% (−10.7 bps), its biggest one-day drop since August 2025, as money flees AI stocks

CNBC quotes at 4:31 PM ET: US10Y 5.083% (−10.7 bps), US30Y 5.423% (−10.1), US5Y 4.897% (−9.5), US2Y 4.755% (−4.7). Kitco called it the 10-year's largest daily decline since August 2025. CME Group noted a 5.08% close and the biggest two-day high-to-low move of 2026 despite hot PPI and retail sales, with CVOL rising alongside. CNBC said investors kept cashing out of tech and hiding in bonds on worries that higher inflation and rates will hit the AI spending boom.

Gold impact: Near term: lower yields cut the opportunity cost of holding bullion, a direct tailwind. The 2-year fell far less (−4.7 bps), so the market still expects the Fed to hike. Longer term: the 10-year is still above 5%; if the AI selloff calms while inflation stays hot, yields can snap back and weigh on gold.
