U.S. stock futures bounce Sunday night – Nasdaq-100 +0.8%, S&P 500 +0.4% as dip buyers return to AI; Goldman flags AI credit spreads at the year's widest

CNBC (Sunday, 6:15 PM ET): futures rebounded after last week's AI rout – at the open Nasdaq-100 futures were +0.4%, Dow futures +0.2% (+110 points) and S&P 500 futures +0.2%; by 7:46 PM ET CNBC quoted Nasdaq-100 futures at 29,628 (+0.80%) and S&P 500 futures at 7,737 (+0.43%). Last week the S&P 500 lost nearly 2% (its worst week since late June), the Nasdaq fell 4.5% and CoreWeave dropped 25%. Goldman Sachs noted Friday that AI and hyperscaler credit spreads are at the widest of the year, with the second-heaviest put volume on record after June. Meta and Tesla report this week.

Gold impact: Near term: a calmer risk mood cools the haven bid and can cap gold near $4,200. If Meta/Tesla disappoint and AI credit keeps widening, money rotates back into Treasuries, pulling yields lower – a tailwind for gold. Longer term: AI-debt risk is another reason for big money to diversify into gold.
