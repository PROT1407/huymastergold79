GoldBearish for gold ↓🕐 Notices late Oct 15 (India); effective Oct 16 (Times of India, 9:56 PM ET Oct 15)

India: major fund houses halt new money into gold ETF funds from Oct 16 after a SEBI nudge – right before Diwali

Reuters (via The Hindu BusinessLine): Kotak, UTI, HDFC and other large Indian fund houses issued notices late Thursday suspending bulk subscriptions to gold ETFs while still allowing redemptions; sources said the move followed a nudge from the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI). CNBC TV18: from Oct 16, ICICI Prudential, HDFC, Aditya Birla, Invesco, DSP, Tata and others stopped taking fresh money into gold ETF fund-of-funds (lump sums, new SIPs, top-ups); existing SIPs and redemptions continue, and the ETFs still trade on exchange. India's September gold imports fell to $1.8 billion from $2.3 billion in August. Times of India: FoFs account for about a third of roughly ₹1.7 lakh crore in gold ETF assets, and industry officials don't see it as a game changer for demand.

Gold impact: Near term: a sign that India, the world's No. 2 gold consumer, wants to curb imports right into festival season dents seasonal demand expectations – a mild negative. Longer term: if curbs widen (import duty is already 15%), Indian physical demand could stay soft; but FoFs are small, so this isn't a trend-setter on its own.
