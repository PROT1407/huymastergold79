China's Q3 GDP grows 4.5%, beating the 4.4% forecast on exports – September retail sales +1.1%, industrial output +5.5%, factory prices +5% – gold barely reacts

CNBC (Sunday, 10:05 PM ET): China's Q3 GDP grew 4.5% y/y (Q2: 4.3%, a three-year low; Reuters poll: 4.4%), putting nine-month growth at 4.6% – inside Beijing's 4.5%–5% target. September retail sales rose 1.1% (est. 0.6%), industrial output 5.5% (in line), urban unemployment 5.2%; fixed-asset investment fell 7.2% year-to-date and property investment 20.6%. The monthly trade surplus hit a record $133.5 billion; producer prices rose 5%, the fastest in over four years, while consumer inflation stays below 1%. NBS: Q3 GDP +1.2% q/q. Spot gold traded around 4,170–4,183 through the release (CNBC XAU=).

Gold impact: Near term: a modest positive for metals demand, but not enough to set a trend – gold is still trading yields and the dollar. Longer term: a firmer China means less stimulus pressure, and 5% factory-gate inflation adds to global goods inflation – another reason central banks keep rates high.
