Gold macro brief, Oct 26, 2026 – Weekend flare-up: Saudi Arabia knocks down Houthi missiles and drones aimed at Riyadh and Aramco's Yanbu sites as markets reopen with post-midterm Iran strike risk in play

Weekend flare-up: Saudi Arabia knocks down Houthi missiles and drones aimed at Riyadh and Aramco's Yanbu sites as markets reopen with post-midterm Iran strike risk in play

War & geopoliticsBearish gold ↓Oct 25, 8:49 AM ET (CNBC)

CNBC: the Saudi-led coalition said it intercepted two drones and a ballistic missile headed for Riyadh, 10 ballistic missiles aimed at the south and a cruise missile targeting Khamis Mushait on Sunday. The Houthis claimed "seven qualitative military operations," naming King Khalid International Airport and Aramco facilities at Yanbu. No damage or casualties were reported, and Riyadh's airport reopened Terminals 1 and 2 the same day (AP). President Trump's remarks last week suggesting the US and Israel are planning fresh strikes on Iran after the Nov. 3 midterms helped push Brent to a six-month high of $116.72 on Friday. None of the weekend news is priced yet.

Gold impact: Near term: the risk is another leg higher in crude at the open – and right now higher oil means higher inflation expectations and yields, which hurts gold more than any haven bid helps it. If no barrels are actually lost, the risk premium can bleed out and ease the pressure. Longer term: Nov. 3 cuts both ways. Strikes that hit energy infrastructure keep inflation sticky; a de-escalation or a new round of talks is what gold needs to trade like a haven again.

🔗 Source: CNBC – Saudi Arabia says it intercepted missiles and drones from Iran-backed Houthis · AP – Iran says messaging continues with the US; Riyadh airport reopens

Fed week: the Street is bracing for a "hawkish pause" – roughly 90% odds of a hold on Oct. 28, but about 70% odds of a quarter-point hike in December

FOMCBearish gold ↓Oct 25, 5:00 PM ET (Yahoo Finance)

Yahoo Finance: the Fed heads into its Oct. 27–28 meeting with inflation running hotter – September CPI at 3.7% y/y, wholesale prices up more than 6%, and core PCE projected above 3%. Former Fed Vice Chair Alan Blinder expects a "hawkish pause" that strongly hints the next move could be a hike. Harvard's Ken Rogoff says the fundamentals call for a hike but the Fed will hold with the midterms less than a week away. Cleveland Fed President Beth Hammack has argued for small hikes now. Traders price nearly 90% odds of a hold this week and 70% odds of a quarter-point hike in December. The statement is due Wednesday at 2:00 PM ET, with Chair Warsh's presser at 2:30 PM ET.

Gold impact: Near term: a hold is priced, so the move comes from the wording. If Warsh keeps December firmly on the table, yields and the dollar stay bid and $4,000 stays a ceiling for gold; if the message leans "we need more data," there's room for a relief bounce. Longer term: the Fed is still in a tightening cycle against sticky inflation, and high real rates remain gold's main headwind until inflation rolls over.

🔗 Source: Yahoo Finance – Fed expected to hold rates steady this week despite hot inflation, but signal they're not done hiking · Investing.com Fed Rate Monitor (CME fed funds futures)

Kitco survey: Wall Street turns bearish after gold's break below $4,000 (41% lower, 24% higher) while Main Street stays more upbeat (42% higher)

Cross-assetBearish gold ↓Oct 23, 10:00 PM ET (Kitco)

Kitco: of 17 analysts surveyed, four (24%) see gold higher this week, seven (41%) see more downside and six (35%) look for sideways trade. In the online poll, 67 of 161 retail voters (42%) expect gains, 41 (26%) expect losses and 53 (33%) expect consolidation. Marc Chandler of Bannockburn Global Forex says the technical tone still looks weak, with initial resistance at $4,065–4,075. Kevin Grady of Phoenix Futures and Options warns that liquidity is thin and stops sit below $4,000. FxPro's Alex Kuptsikevich calls the drop overdone and sees room for a corrective bounce, noting the $3,950–4,100 area has drawn steady buying for more than a year.

Gold impact: Near term: bearish pros plus thin pre-Fed liquidity is a recipe for whipsaws around $4,000 – wait for confirmation instead of picking a bottom; bulls need $4,065–4,075 back to have a case. Longer term: when the pros and the crowd disagree this much, expect volatility; the setup only improves once crude cools and hike bets fade.

🔗 Source: Kitco News – Wall Street doubts gold's near-term strength, Main Street maintains bullish minority
