Post-CPI New York settle: spot gold ~$4,093 (−1.5%) · silver −6.5% · DXY +0.4% – third straight down day

Cross-assetBearish gold ↓New York settle Oct 14; CNBC post-session update; GMT Eight late-NY marks

CNBC (post-session Oct 14 update): spot gold fell 1.5% to $4,093.03/oz – lowest since Oct 7; December futures −1.5% to $4,115.50. Spot silver −6.5% to $55.60; platinum −3.5% (~$1,604), palladium −2.7% (~$1,095). The dollar index rose about 0.4%; the 10-year yield also climbed. High Ridge Futures' David Meger: hotter-than-target CPI → higher hike odds → firmer USD pressure on gold. CNBC cited CME FedWatch at ~35% for an October hike (from ~22% pre-print) and ~90% odds of at least one hike before year-end. Julius Baer's Carsten Menke: longer term, central-bank buying and US debt concerns should support gold again. GMT Eight (late NY): spot ~$4,103.80 (−1.37%), COMEX ~$4,121.90 (−1.46%).

Gold impact: Near term: hot core CPI → hike odds up → firmer USD/yields → gold lower, textbook; silver sold harder as both a monetary and industrial metal. Medium term: if DXY holds above 102.5 and December hike odds stay elevated, sustained rebounds need a fresh geopolitical shock. Next up: PPI and retail sales at 8:30 AM ET Oct 15.

End-of-day FedWatch Oct 14: October hold 69% · 25 bp hike 31% – December: hold 11% · +25 bp 63% · +50 bp 26%

FedBearish gold ↓Investing.com updated Oct 14, 7:15 PM ET

Investing.com Fed Rate Monitor (CME 30-Day Fed Funds futures), updated 7:15 PM ET Oct 14 (after the CPI reaction): Oct 28 meeting – hold at 3.75–4.00% = 69.0% (prior day 79.3%), hike to 4.00–4.25% = 31.0% (prior day 20.7%). Dec 9 – hold 11.4%; cumulative +25 bp = 62.7%; cumulative +50 bp = 25.9%. Versus the intraday spike (CNBC earlier cited ~54% for an October hike), markets dialed back an immediate October move but still price a high chance of more tightening before year-end. CNBC's post-session gold wrap put October hike odds near 35% and at-least-one-hike-by-year-end near 90% – same direction as the Investing.com snapshot.

Gold impact: Near term: ~31% October hike odds are still well above the pre-CPI ~20–22% → supports the dollar and yields → headwind for gold. Medium term: 'higher for longer' (December almost priced for more tightening, ~26% chance of +50 bp cumulative) keeps real-rate pressure on gold unless PPI/retail sales cool or geopolitics reignites a safe-haven bid.

Oil settles higher Oct 14: Brent $106.06 · WTI $95.09 – Aramco attack + IEA diesel warning

OilNeutralNYMEX/ICE settle Oct 14; CNBC session/post-session wrap

CNBC: Brent rose 37 cents to settle at $106.06/bbl; WTI gained 95 cents to $95.09. Saudi Energy Ministry confirmed an Aramco administrative building in Dhahran was attacked (no casualties). The IEA's October Oil Market Report warned diesel constraints could run into 2027; fresh attacks on Saudi Arabia threaten the supply-side recovery. CENTCOM rejected the IRGC Navy commander's claim that Iran controls the Strait of Hormuz. Paul Tudor Jones (CNBC Squawk Box): still long oil, sees another $20–25 upside if the Iran conflict isn't eased before US midterms.

Gold impact: Near term: firmer oil → hotter energy CPI/PPI → supports Fed-tightening bets → headwind for gold via the dollar and yields; Gulf risk is a mild safe-haven offset. The two forces clashed on Oct 14 – USD/yields won, so gold fell even as crude rose. Medium term: if Brent holds above $105–106 and Hormuz/Houthi risks escalate, the Fed struggles to 'skip' for long → rate pressure on gold unless the war bid is strong enough to offset.

Fed's Bowman: time to wait and see how markets and the economy evolve after the September hike

FedNeutralCited in CNBC's Oct 14 post-session gold update

CNBC (post-CPI gold wrap Oct 14): Fed Vice Chair for Supervision Michelle Bowman said the central bank has time to wait and see how financial markets and the economy evolve after September's rate rise before deciding on further action. That 'no rush' tone softens part of the hawkish post-CPI impulse and fits the end-of-day FedWatch pullback in October hike odds from the intraday spike toward ~31–35%.

Gold impact: Near term: Bowman's cautious tone mildly supports an October 'skip' – a bit less USD pressure on gold than the peak CPI reaction. Medium term: she didn't rule out another hike; Thursday's PPI and retail sales, plus Chair Warsh, are the next tests.
