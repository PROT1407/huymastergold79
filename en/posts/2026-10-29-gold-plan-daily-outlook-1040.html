H79 Gold Plan – Daily Outlook, Oct 29: Fed Spike Rejected at 4,075–4,085 – Sell the Rally, 3,900 Still the Target

The Big Picture:
The Fed held at 3.75%–4.00% as expected, but the real headline is the two dissents: Governor Cook and Cleveland's Hammack wanted another quarter-point hike now. The statement kept "inflation remains elevated," upgraded the growth read, and Chair Warsh refused to tip his hand on December. Call it a hawkish hold.
Bonds got the message — the 10-year finished around 5.03%, and FedWatch still prices better than 80% odds of a December hike. Crude adds fuel: the Houthi missile claim on Saudi Arabia's Ras Tanura refinery lifted Brent to $115.32 and WTI to $102.78. Hot oil keeps inflation sticky, sticky inflation keeps the Fed leaning tight, and tight policy keeps yields a headwind for a metal that pays nothing.
Gold's own tape says the rest. The Warsh presser squeezed spot to ~4,079 — right into 4,075–4,085 — and it got sold straight back to the low 4,040s. The overnight bounce stalled at 4,066, a lower high. Yields and the dollar are flat-to-softer this morning, and gold still can't hold a bid. Sellers own this tape. Gold is down more than 2% in October, on track for a second straight monthly loss.
Yesterday's call — sell rallies, target 3,900 — is playing out. This morning's PCE is the last big hurdle of the week.

Structure:
Lower highs are intact: ~4,141 (Oct 21) → 4,079 (post-FOMC) → 4,066 (overnight). Each bounce is shorter than the last.
Below, 4,028 is a double low (Wednesday's low and the Fed-candle low). Lose it and 4,000 then 3,975 open up; real demand doesn't show until 3,900–3,918.
As long as we're under 4,105, every pop is a selling opportunity.

Scenarios:
🔻 Base (~55%): a bounce into 4,060–4,085 gets sold, 4,028 gives way → 4,015 → 3,975, then 3,900. A hot PCE speeds this up and could take out 4,000 by the New York afternoon.
🔺 Alt (~30%): a soft PCE squeezes gold into 4,080–4,100. Below the 4,105 ceiling, that's a better place to sell — wait for the rejection candle.
⚠️ Invalidation (~15%): a daily close above 4,105 shelves the short; next up is 4,123–4,141.

Key Levels:
Resistance: 4,059–4,066 · 4,075–4,085 (rejected zone) · 4,100–4,105 (ceiling)
Support: 4,028 · 4,015 · 4,000 · 3,975 · 3,900

Data Calendar – PCE Today:
8:30 AM ET (pre-market): Q3 GDP (consensus 3.2%, prior 2.2%) · September core PCE (+0.3% m/m, 3.1% y/y; prior +0.2% / 3.0%) · headline PCE (3.7% y/y; prior 3.4%) · jobless claims (200K; prior 194K).
9:15 AM ET: ECB decision, just ahead of the New York open.
Core at 0.4% or hotter → 4,000 likely breaks. Core at 0.2% or softer → a squeeze toward 4,080–4,100; that's where you sell, not where you chase.
