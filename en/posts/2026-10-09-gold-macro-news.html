Gold macro brief, Oct 9, 2026 – 10-year real yield (TIPS) at 2.91% – well above where it started the year

10-year real yield (TIPS) at 2.91% – well above where it started the year

YieldsBearish gold ↓Close Oct 9 (≈ 5:00 PM ET)

The 10-year TIPS real yield closed at 2.91% on Oct 9, 2026 (1.94% in early April; up nearly 0.5 pt in September). The 10-year breakeven inflation rate is about 2.33%.

Gold impact: High real yields raise the opportunity cost of holding gold – in theory gold's main headwind; central-bank buying has weakened that relationship since 2022.
