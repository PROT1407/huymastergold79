H79 Gold Plan – Daily Outlook, Oct 28: Selling Pressure Still in Charge – Sell Strength at 4,062–4,068, Path Open Toward 3,900

The Big Picture: Spot gold is chopping around 4,040–4,060 ahead of the Fed (~2:00 PM ET Oct 28, press conference ~2:30 PM ET). The dollar is firm near 102.3–102.4 and the 10-year sits around 5.0% — still the main weight on a non-yielding metal. Softer oil vs. recent highs (WTI near $100, Brent still elevated) eases the near-term inflation scare a bit, but it doesn't flip the higher-for-longer story. Markets lean toward a hold this meeting, yet FedWatch still prices a solid chance of a December hike. Geopolitics and crude will whip prices around; the durable force remains USD and yields. My read: selling pressure stays the dominant tape, and it can persist through the Fed print if the message stays hawkish or DXY/yields refuse to ease.

Structure: From the ~4,247 swing high, gold has printed lower highs — fail near 4,123, then the latest bounce rejected into supply at 4,062–4,068 (Asia high ~4,069). That's sell-the-strength structure, not a trend flip. Technical expectation: path still open toward the 3,900–3,9xx zone (pivots near 3,918 / 3,898–3,900) as long as rallies keep failing under old supply.

Scenarios:
🔻 Base (~55%): bounce into 4,062–4,068 gets sold; a break of 4,012 / 4,000 / 3,990 opens 3,918 → 3,900–3,9xx.
🔺 Alt (~30%): a dovish Fed surprise or oil/geo spike holds gold above 4,000 and retests 4,068 then 4,100+; only a firm close above ~4,078 softens the short bias.
⚠️ Tail (~15%): a clean break of 3,990–3,980 slides fast into 3,918 / 3,900 into the event — size down around the Fed.

Key Levels:
Resistance/supply: 4,062–4,068 · ~4,100–4,123 · 4,247.
Support: 4,012 · 4,000 · 3,990 · 3,918 · 3,898–3,900.

Fed Calendar: Fed decision ~2:00 PM ET Oct 28 · Chair press conference ~2:30 PM ET. Markets lean toward a hold this meeting; the real story is the tone and the path into December. Into the print: size down, bank TP1 and move stops to breakeven — don't chase Fed candles.
