Gold snaps back about 1% to reclaim $4,050 as crude cools – all eyes on Wednesday's Fed

Cross-assetBullish gold ↑Oct 27, 12:41 PM ET (CNBC)

CNBC (Reuters): after dipping to $3,987.14 intraday, spot gold was up 0.9% at $4,051.78 an ounce by 12:41 PM ET Tuesday, with December futures 0.69% higher at $4,066.40. Zaner Metals' Peter Grant says softer oil eases inflation worries and takes some edge off Fed hawkishness. American Gold Exchange's Jim Wyckoff warns the flip side: if crude turns higher again, central banks tighten – and that's bearish gold. CNBC's XAU= quote was 4,057.80 at 7:25 PM ET, with a 3,990–4,065 range on the day. Silver rose 0.8% to $54.37 after touching a near one-year low.

Gold impact: Near term: defending $4,000 and closing above 4,050 is a constructive pre-Fed tell; 4,060–4,065 is first resistance, and a slip back under 4,000 would mean the bounce failed. Longer term: a Reuters poll shows analysts trimming their 2026 gold forecasts for a second straight quarter on high yields and a strong dollar, while still pencilling in a 2027 recovery – the bigger trend hinges on the Fed's message.

Crude settles lower for a second day: Brent $113.88, WTI $99.90 – Ras Tanura missile headline fades fast

War & geopoliticsNeutralOct 27, ~2:30 PM ET (settlement)

CNBC/Reuters: Brent settled down $1.93 (−1.67%) at $113.88 a barrel and WTI fell $1.86 (−1.83%) to $99.90. Crude spiked on Iranian media reports that Saudi Arabia's Ras Tanura refinery was targeted by missiles, then rolled over after Iran's Supreme Leader Mojtaba Khamenei said the state of "neither war nor peace" needs to end. Houthi attacks on Saudi Arabia continue, and October has been the worst month for attacks on shipping around the Strait of Hormuz. UBS lifted its year-end Brent call to about $110 from $100.

Gold impact: Near term: softer crude is a tailwind for gold – less inflation pressure, less reason for the Fed to hike – but any fresh Gulf strike can lift oil and the dollar together. Longer term: multi-year-low global inventories point to higher-for-longer oil, which keeps sticky inflation and high rates in play as a headwind for bullion.

🔗 Source: CNBC – Oil falls as Middle East talks raise de-escalation hopes · Reuters via Investing.com – Oil falls almost $2 as Middle East talks ease supply worries

10-year yield holds near 4.99%, 2-year at 4.735% – dollar index hits two-week high above 102 after upbeat US data

YieldsBearish gold ↓Oct 27, 3:35 PM ET (WSJ)

WSJ: the 10-year yield rose 0.4 bp to 4.992% and the 2-year added 2.6 bp to 4.735% by 3:35 PM ET, both up three sessions in a row. September durable goods orders and October consumer confidence both beat forecasts, while the 5-year auction cleared at a lower yield than last month. FXStreet: the dollar index pushed above 102 to a two-week high as traders brace for a hawkish hold; ING and HSBC both see the dollar supported on dips.

Gold impact: Near term: yields knocking on 5% and a two-week high in the dollar cap gold's bounce – a decisive 10-year break above 5% after the Fed would put $4,000 back in play. Longer term: a resilient US economy gives the Fed room to keep rates higher for longer, so the opportunity cost of holding gold stays elevated.
