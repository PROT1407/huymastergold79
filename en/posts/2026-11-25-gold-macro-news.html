Gold, silver extend losses as tech stocks slide, Fed inflation test looms

GoldBearish gold ↓Tuesday, November 24, 2026, 17:32 EST

Spot gold was trading near $3,552.50 an ounce, down $81.70, or 2.25%, while silver was trading near $40.790, down $2.250, or 5.22%, on the session. Gold traded as low as $3,546.00 during the day. The Conference Board's November consumer confidence index surged 11.4 points to 103.3 from an upwardly revised 91.9 in October.

Gold impact: Stronger consumer confidence and broader portfolio liquidation pressured gold in the near term, while persistent inflation risks and a hawkish Federal Reserve maintain downward pressure over the longer term.

HSBC, J.P. Morgan and StoneX complete first live trades using Pooled Gold Interests (PGI)

Cross-assetNeutralTuesday, November 24, 2026, 17:32 EST

Gold's digital evolution passed a major milestone after HSBC, J.P. Morgan and StoneX conducted wholesale-market transactions using Pooled Gold Interests (PGI) for the first time. Loco London physical 400-ounce Good Delivery gold bars were placed into the Wholesale Digital Gold ecosystem, and PGI were created.

Gold impact: Digitalization enhances gold's liquidity, allowing fractional ownership and more efficient use of physical gold as collateral across modern financial markets.
