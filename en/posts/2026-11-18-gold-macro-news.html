India’s gold demand boosted by lower prices in October, with jewelry, ETFs, and imports strong – WGC’s Chacko

GoldNeutralTuesday, November 17, 2026, 23:42 EST

Following a relatively stable first half of October, gold prices fell sharply, ending the month 6.3% lower at US$3,913/oz, marking a second consecutive monthly decline. Retailer estimates indicate that demand in value terms increased 15%-25% y/y, while Indian gold ETFs recorded positive net inflows for the fifth consecutive month in October.

Gold impact: In the short term, lower prices stimulate seasonal physical demand for jewelry and bars and coins in key markets like India. Over the long term, this physical absorption supports a firm structural floor for global gold prices.

Goldwill reclaim $5,600/oz in 2028 as rate cuts bring Western investors back to precious metals – Goldman Sachs

GoldBullish gold ↑Tuesday, November 17, 2026, 23:42 EST

Gold will reclaim its all-time high above $5,600 per ounce by the end of 2028 after the Federal Reserve begins cutting interest rates and Western investors return to the precious metals market, according to analysts at Goldman Sachs. They see gold at $4,650 per ounce by year-end 2027, driven by continued central bank purchases averaging 60 tonnes per month.

Gold impact: In the near term, ongoing Fed rate hikes pose downside volatility risks for private investors. Over the long term, structural central bank demand and an anticipated shift toward rate cuts provide strong upward potential for gold.
