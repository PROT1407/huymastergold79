But risk appetite soured into the open: Nasdaq futures are down more than 1% on worries about AI companies' heavy borrowing, with S&P 500 futures off 0.3%, and the dollar ticked back up from 102.03 to 102.13. With PPI and retail sales minutes away, longs took profits into the 4,152–4,160 resistance cluster – the BB90 mid on the 45-minute, 2-hour, 3-hour and 1-hour charts.

The 8:30 AM ET game plan (PPI + retail sales): Into the data (8:00–8:45 AM ET): no new positions. ForexFactory consensus: PPI +0.6% m/m (prior 0.4%), core PPI +0.3% (prior 0.2%), retail sales +0.3% (prior 1.2%), core retail sales +0.6% (prior 1.4%), alongside jobless claims (199K) and the Philly Fed (28.2). Scenario 1 – hot PPI / strong spending: the dollar and yields pop, gold likely breaks 4,136–4,140 (50% Fib · 30m mid/MA89) and heads to 4,114 (daily pivot), then the 4,092–4,102 buy zone. Scenario 2 – soft PPI / weak spending: gold bounces into 4,158–4,171, which is the high-probability H79 SELL area if a 45m/30m candle pokes above the mid and closes red back below it. Only a 1H close above 4,171 (4H mid, 78.6% Fib) cancels the short and opens 4,197–4,202.
