Gold macro brief, Oct 12, 2026 – Zelenskyy: Russia broke the 'energy ceasefire'; Brent settles at $106.65, Iran still hasn't reopened Hormuz

Zelenskyy: Russia broke the 'energy ceasefire'; Brent settles at $106.65, Iran still hasn't reopened Hormuz

War & geopoliticsBearish gold ↓Zelenskyy's remarks: Monday Oct 12 (early morning to midday ET); oil settled 2:30 PM ET Oct 12

Hours after President Trump announced a Russia–Ukraine 'energy ceasefire', President Zelenskyy said Russia hit six Ukrainian energy facilities on Monday Oct 12 alone; the Kremlin said contacts are ongoing (CNBC). Meanwhile the Houthis kept firing drones and missiles at Saudi Arabia after the Riyadh airport attack. Oil flipped from losses to gains: Brent settled +1.8% at $106.65/bbl, WTI +2% at $93.74. Kitco quoted Iranian security official Mohsen Rezaei: the latest US response to Iran's seven conditions for reopening the Strait of Hormuz is 'not enough', and there will be no talks until the conditions are met.

Gold impact: A drawn-out war creates safe-haven demand, but the 'oil up → inflation → Fed hikes → strong dollar and yields' channel is winning: gold couldn't hold its early one-week high. Any sign Hormuz reopens would pull oil lower and could help gold recover.

🔗 Source: CNBC – Brent tops $106 after Saudi airport attack, Ukraine says Russia violated energy ceasefire · CNBC – Zelenskyy accuses Russia of violating Trump's energy ceasefire

Dollar holds near an 18-month high (DXY 102.57); gold ends Monday down 0.6% around $4,169

Cross-assetBearish gold ↓CNBC posted Oct 12, 12:06 AM ET; gold quoted after the US close, ~5:00 PM ET Oct 12

CNBC (Reuters): the dollar index rose 0.3% to 102.57 – just under last week's 18-month high – on safe-haven flows as the Middle East conflict escalated and a weak euro (French fiscal worries, the France–Germany spread at 140 bps). CFTC data show net dollar longs rose to about $36B in the week to Oct 7, from $8.4B three weeks earlier. CME FedWatch: 81% odds of an October hold. Kitco: after Monday's close spot gold was around $4,168.90/oz (−0.59%), silver $60.12 (−0.96%); the market sees about 87.7% odds of at least one Fed hike by December.

Gold impact: A strong dollar makes gold pricier for non-US buyers and goes hand in hand with Fed tightening bets → downside pressure. Kitco: 4,175 then 4,155 need to hold; resistance 4,221–4,227. September CPI (Wed Oct 14, 8:30 AM ET) is the main test.

🔗 Source: CNBC – Dollar hovers near 18-month high as Gulf unrest, U.S. inflation keep investors on edge · Kitco – Gold, silver soften as markets price in December Fed hike risk (PM Report 12/10)

Fed's Hammack: policy needs to be tighter – hike in 'small but quick steps'

FedBearish gold ↓CNBC posted Oct 12, 1:08 PM ET

Cleveland Fed President Beth Hammack (a 2026 FOMC voter) told the NABE conference on Oct 12 that 'a restrictive policy stance is appropriate' to get inflation back to 2%, and she favors 'small adjustments, made relatively quickly' rather than waiting and having to correct harder later. She warned persistent high inflation raises the risk of an inflation psychology taking hold; August PCE was 3.4%, core PCE 3%. Per CME FedWatch (cited by CNBC), the market sees over 78% odds of an October hold but nearly 88% odds of a December hike.

Gold impact: A hawkish tone reinforces expectations of more Fed hikes → supports yields and the dollar, raising the opportunity cost of holding gold. Pressure could build if Wednesday's CPI (Oct 14, 8:30 AM ET) comes in hot.

🔗 Source: CNBC – Fed's Hammack: higher rates could be needed · Cleveland Fed – Speech, Oct 12, 2026 (PDF)

Iran hits another supertanker in Hormuz; dollar near 18-month high, gold loses $4,200

War & geopoliticsBearish gold ↓Monday morning Oct 12, Tehran time (≈ late night Oct 11 to early Oct 12 ET); gold quoted ahead of the NY open ≈ 8:00–9:00 AM ET Oct 12

Iran's IRGC Navy said Monday morning Oct 12 it struck a supertanker trying to transit the Strait of Hormuz with its transponder off, setting it on fire (AFP) – the third such incident in two days per the IRGC. Kitco: spot gold briefly topped $4,200 in Asian trade before slipping to about $4,156 ahead of the New York open (down nearly 1%); DXY around 102.2, near its highest in about 18 months; Brent around $105, WTI above $92/bbl. The Treasury market was closed for Columbus Day.

Gold impact: War risk supports safe-haven demand, but high oil keeps inflation elevated → Fed hike expectations (about 85% odds of a hike by December per Kitco) and a strong dollar are winning out – gold under pressure ahead of CPI.

🔗 Source: Kitco – Gold struggles as US dollar nears 18-month high (12/10/2026) · AFP (qua The Hindu) – Iran Guards say struck vessel in Hormuz

Gold reverses lower as oil and diesel rebound on Houthi attacks on Saudi Arabia

War & geopoliticsBearish gold ↓London and New York sessions Oct 12 (early morning to midday ET)

On Monday Oct 12 gold gave up its early gains as crude rose and US diesel recovered (after a 3% drop Friday to $4.74/gal), even though President Trump eased sanctions on Russian diesel and announced a Russia–Ukraine 'energy ceasefire'. Fears of a wider conflict grew after the Houthis claimed attacks on Riyadh airport and two Saudi Aramco facilities; Trump suggested the US could join Saudi Arabia in striking the Houthis.

Gold impact: The 'oil → inflation → rates' channel: Middle East tension lifts crude and reinforces the case for Fed hikes – bearish for gold. Heraeus: 'gold remains under pressure as Middle East tensions keep pushing oil prices higher'.

🔗 Source: BullionVault – Gold News 12/10/2026

Trump announces a Russia–Ukraine 'energy ceasefire'; gold edges up around $4,200

War & geopoliticsNeutralGold quoted 00:30 GMT Oct 12 = 8:30 PM ET Oct 11 (Trump's post: Sunday Oct 11, US time)

On Sunday Oct 11 President Trump posted that Russia and Ukraine had agreed to an 'energy ceasefire' effective immediately, without giving details. Ukraine said it would comply if Russia does and accused Russia of still hitting energy facilities in Kyiv and Kharkiv; the Kremlin said contacts are ongoing. Early in Asian trade on Oct 12, XAU/USD rose 0.3% to $4,206.86/oz (00:30 GMT).

Gold impact: Hopes of easing fuel-supply pressure (lower inflation) offset Middle East risk; ANZ noted inflation risk could cap gains if it pushes the Fed to tighten further.
