The post-midterm rally came from a weaker dollar and lower yields, plus the market cutting the odds of a December Fed hike from about 82% to about 60%. Gold bounced from 3,920 to 4,144.9 late on Nov 4 (ET). But that high landed right on the top of the descending channel and the base of the old 4,146–4,206 supply (the mid-October highs), and it was sold. Price couldn't hold 4,100 and is back near 4,060 in Asian trade. The 4H chart is still a descending parallel channel. The liquidity sweep under the 3,870 low at the end of October produced a V-shaped bounce, but the new high at 4,144.9 is still below 4,205.6 (Oct 9) and 4,224 (Oct 2). 4H RSI has cooled from ~70 to ~55 – momentum is fading right at resistance. This is a bounce inside a downtrend, not a reversal. The Asian-session drop left a fair value gap (FVG) at 4,072–4,097. A retest of that gap that gets rejected is the spot to sell with the channel, targeting 4,005 → 3,900, then 3,689 and the long-term 3,600–3,500. Friday's payrolls (8:30 AM ET) are the trigger: a strong jobs number lifting yields speeds this up; a very weak one could squeeze price back into supply first.
