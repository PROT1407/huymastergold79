Gold macro brief, Sep 16, 2026 – FOMC hikes 25 bps to 3.75–4.00% (12–0)

FOMC hikes 25 bps to 3.75–4.00% (12–0)

FOMCBearish gold ↓Sep 16, 2:00 PM ET

At its Sep 15–16 meeting the FOMC unanimously raised the federal funds target range by 25 bps to 3.75–4.00%, effective Sep 17. Statement: inflation remains elevated and the Fed 'will return inflation to price stability'.

Gold impact: Fed tightening lifts yields and supports the dollar – usually a headwind for gold.

🔗 Source: Federal Reserve – FOMC statement 16/09/2026

September dot plot: 4.1% median for end-2026 – one more hike

FOMCBearish gold ↓Sep 16, 2:00 PM ET

Per the Sep 16 Summary of Economic Projections: the median end-2026 rate is 4.1%, 2027: 4.1%, 2028: 3.9%, 2029: 3.6%, longer run 3.2%. 16 of 18 participants see one more hike in 2026; 4 see two.

Gold impact: A 'higher for longer' path keeps real yields high – bearish for gold if it plays out.

🔗 Source: Federal Reserve – Summary of Economic Projections · J.P. Morgan AM – FOMC Statement September 2026
