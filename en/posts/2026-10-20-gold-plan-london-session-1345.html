H79 Gold Plan – London Session, Oct 20: A Softer Dollar Can't Lift Gold Past 4,146 – Sell 4,140–4,146, Buy 4,116–4,122

Asia recap: the short paid in full, the long was a beat late: The 4,140–4,146 short played out as drawn: filled around 4,143, TP1 at 4,134 (+$9), then TP2 at 4,125 (+$18). Gold did tag the buy zone, but the 1H closed red, so we stayed flat and missed the $30 bounce. The tell was there: a 45m bar closed red outside the 90 band, and the very next 45m bar flipped green and closed back inside – that was a buyable setup with room to target. Waiting on the 1H close is safer, but it was one beat too late.

Why a softer dollar didn't help: On paper, this morning favored gold: the dollar index is down 0.2% at 101.77, the 10-year eased to 5.12% from 5.14%, Asian equities are red (Kospi −2.8%, Euro Stoxx 50 futures −1%), and the Houthis claimed strikes on Saudi energy, airport and military targets. Gold did pop to 4,145 around 11:47 PM ET – Reuters called it 'inching up on a softer dollar' – then got sold back to 4,118 inside two hours. My read: the market still expects a December hike (89% on CME FedWatch) even with 82% betting on a hold Oct. 28, and BOJ Governor Ueda said underlying inflation is 'quite close' to 2%, leaving the door open to more Japanese hikes – global yields don't have room to fall. When good news can't lift the price, I trust the price.

Bias: 🔻 Short-term bearish – a softer dollar couldn't hold gold above 4,146; sell a bounce into 4,140–4,146, buy the 4,116–4,122 double bottom only on a confirming close
