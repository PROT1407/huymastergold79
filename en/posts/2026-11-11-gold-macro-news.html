Gold macro brief, Nov 11, 2026 – Spot gold drops ~2% to near $4,055 after hotter October CPI; 10-year yield climbs to ~4.94%

Spot gold drops ~2% to near $4,055 after hotter October CPI; 10-year yield climbs to ~4.94%

GoldBearish gold ↓Nov 10, 8:30 AM ET (CPI); Kitco AM wrap that morning

Kitco AM, early U.S. trading Tuesday: spot gold near $4,055.30, down $84.10 (−2.03%); spot silver near $51.70 (−3.01%). October CPI rose 0.4% (consensus 0.3%; September +0.6%) and core CPI 0.3% (consensus 0.2%), the third straight 0.3% core print. The 10-year yield climbed toward 4.94%, the 2-year toward 4.69%, and the dollar firmed. Per Kitco, futures priced about an 84% chance of a 25 bp hike at the Dec. 8–9 meeting before the release; after CPI and Waller's remarks a quarter-point move was almost fully priced, with some probability on a larger one.

Gold impact: Near term: sticky 0.3% core inflation lifted yields and the dollar and stalled gold's post-election bounce; Kitco flags support at $4,051.45 then $4,021.85, resistance at $4,088.51 and $4,147.62. Bigger picture: further Fed tightening is a heavy headwind for bullion; Friday's PPI is the next inflation test.

Fed's Waller and Logan signal readiness to hike higher and faster, with 50 bps on the table for December

FedBearish gold ↓Nov 10, morning ET

Kitco: Fed Governor Waller said he leans toward a 25 bp hike in December but that if October core CPI ran around 0.3% or higher, a 50 bp hike should be considered — 'Hiking only 25 basis points would put our credibility at risk.' Dallas Fed's Logan said the FOMC needs at least two more 25 bp increases and may be able to move faster this winter. CPI then printed above consensus, the condition Waller flagged.

Gold impact: Near term: hawkish signals from two Fed officials reinforce yields and the dollar, weighing on gold. Bigger picture: if the hiking path stretches into 2027, non-yielding gold stays under pressure; watch Friday's PPI and further Fed speakers.

China adds 26 tonnes of gold in October, reserves reach 2,435 t — just 2 t behind France

Central banksBullish gold ↑October data; Kitco, Nov 10 ET

Citing World Gold Council analyst Krishan Gopaul, Kitco reports China bought 26 tonnes of gold in October, lifting official reserves to 2,435 tonnes, just 2 tonnes behind France.

Gold impact: Near term: official-sector buying is a psychological cushion while gold sells off on CPI. Bigger picture: central-bank demand remains a key pillar of support.

Brent dips below $90 for the first time since August as Trump says more Iran strikes unnecessary; U.S. naval blockade stays

War & geopoliticsNeutralNov 10, early U.S. trading

Kitco AM: Brent briefly fell below $90.00 before recovering toward $90.50; WTI traded near $85.30 (prior settles $92.57 and $86.85). President Trump said Monday additional U.S. strikes on Iran were not necessary at present after appeals from regional governments including Qatar; Washington keeps its naval blockade and no comprehensive deal on maritime security, sanctions relief or Iran's nuclear activities has been reached.

Gold impact: Near term: cheaper oil eases cost-push inflation but does not offset the hot core CPI for gold. Bigger picture: Hormuz and U.S.–Iran talks remain a key swing factor for oil, inflation and rate expectations.
