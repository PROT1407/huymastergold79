Early New York: gold holds green around $4,187 as the dollar, yields and oil all back off ahead of CPI

The decider is CPI at 8:30 AM ET Wednesday: a hotter core print could flip gold lower on December hike bets; a cooler print opens the way above 4,227. Bigger picture: Gulf risk keeps a safe-haven bid, but yields above 5% are still a major headwind.

Oil snaps a 3-day rally: Brent back to ~$105.20–105.80 on more Russian diesel and a Saudi pipeline bypassing Hormuz

OilBullish gold ↑Asian session, evening Oct 12 ET

Brent fell 0.8–1.4% to about $105.20–105.80/bbl, WTI ~$93 in Asian trade on Oct 13. Russia agreed to export more diesel at President Trump's request; the Saudi energy minister said converting a gas pipeline to carry oil could add 1.2M bpd within 7–8 weeks, shipped via the Red Sea to avoid Hormuz. Risks remain: Houthi attacks on Saudi Arabia, tankers hit in Hormuz. Cleveland Fed's Hammack (Bloomberg) favors smaller, more frequent rate moves.

Gold impact: Lower oil → cooler inflation expectations → supportive for gold near term. If Middle East tensions push crude back up, the inflation-to-hawkish-Fed channel will weigh on gold again.

BlackRock goes overweight Treasuries for the first time in ~6 years, says markets overprice Fed tightening

YieldsBullish gold ↑CNBC posted Oct 13, 7:56 AM ET

CNBC: BlackRock (over $15.3 trillion AUM) upgraded US Treasuries to overweight and cut US equities to neutral. IMF warns sovereign yields could spike in a 'disorderly' way on public debt, inflation and AI borrowing

BondsNeutralCNBC posted Oct 12, 11:19 PM ET

CNBC: the IMF's Global Financial Stability Report, released Oct 13 in Bangkok, warned record debt leaves bond markets 'vulnerable to fiscal and inflation shocks'; hedge funds' share of Treasury holdings rose to about 9% (2025) from 4% (2022), raising the risk of a 'disorderly unwind'. AI hyperscalers now account for 10% of global investment-grade bond issuance (from under 1% in 2024), competing with governments for capital.

Gold impact: Near term: a yield spike pressures gold (higher opportunity cost, stronger dollar) and can trigger selling of assets to raise cash. Bigger picture: public-debt risk and eroding trust in government bonds are core reasons central banks and investors hold gold as a safe asset.
