Vietnam gold market: the SJC premium

Vietnam is one of Asia's largest retail gold markets, and its benchmark SJC bars trade at a persistent premium to world prices because bar supply is tightly controlled by the State Bank of Vietnam. Why the premium matters

  • Local demand gauge: a widening premium means local buyers are paying up relative to London/New York – often during panic buying or supply squeezes.
  • Mean reversion: when policy adds bar supply or demand cools, the premium compresses, so local prices can fall even if spot is flat.
  • Double exposure: a local buyer carries both the XAU/USD move and the premium move – plus a wide dealer bid/ask spread.
